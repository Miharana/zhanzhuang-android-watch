Privacy policy — 站桩 · Zhan Zhuang

Effective date: 1 August 2026

站桩 · Zhan Zhuang is a quiet timer for Zhan Zhuang practice. This policy applies to the Android phone and Wear OS watch apps with package name app.zhanzhuang.timer.

Local data

The app stores timer settings and session history locally on the phone. A paired watch keeps the session data needed to continue a timer and sends completed session information to the paired phone after reconnection. If you grant optional watch heart-rate permission, it can record informational heart-rate samples during that session. This is not medical advice.

The app has no account system, developer-operated cloud storage, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or sale of personal data.

Optional Health Connect writing

If you explicitly grant write permissions, the phone app can write a completed mindfulness session to Health Connect. On devices where that record type is unavailable, it writes an exercise-session fallback. Available heart-rate samples can also be written. The app does not read Health Connect data or health data from other apps. Refusing or revoking this permission does not stop the local timer.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you clear the app's storage in Android system settings or remove the app and its data. Records you chose to write to Health Connect are managed in Health Connect and can be removed there.

Permissions and contact

The app requests only timer, notifications/haptics, optional foreground health session, optional watch heart-rate, and optional Health Connect write permissions. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or internet access.

Use the public contact method shown in the app's Google Play listing for privacy questions. The published privacy-policy URL is https://zhanzhuang-privacy.artlinx.workers.dev. A verified public developer contact route must still be set in Play Console before publication; this repository intentionally does not invent one.
