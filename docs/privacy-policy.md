# Privacy policy — 站桩 · Zhan Zhuang

Effective date: 1 August 2026

站桩 · Zhan Zhuang is a quiet timer for Zhan Zhuang practice. This policy applies to the Android phone and Wear OS watch apps with package name `app.zhanzhuang.timer`.

## Data the app keeps locally

The app stores your timer configuration and session history locally on your phone. A paired watch stores the session data needed to continue a timer and sends completed session information to the paired phone when it can reconnect. If you grant the optional heart-rate permission on a Wear OS watch, the watch can record informational heart-rate samples during that session. This information is not medical advice.

The app has no account system and does not send your data to a developer-operated cloud service. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or sell personal data.

## Optional Health Connect writing

Health Connect access is optional. When you explicitly grant write permissions, the phone app can write a completed mindfulness session to Health Connect. On devices where that record type is unavailable, it writes an exercise-session fallback. Available heart-rate samples can also be written. The app does not read health data from Health Connect or from other apps. Refusing or later revoking this permission leaves the basic local timer available.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain on the phone or paired watch until you clear the app's storage in Android system settings or remove the app and its data. Data previously written to Health Connect is governed by your Health Connect controls and can be removed there. Uninstalling the app does not promise to delete records you separately chose to write to Health Connect.

## Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed for its timer, notifications/haptics, optional foreground health session, optional heart-rate collection on the watch, and optional Health Connect writing. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or internet access.

## Contact

For a privacy question, use the public contact method shown in the app's Google Play listing. The published privacy-policy URL is <https://zhanzhuang-privacy.artlinx.workers.dev>. A verified public developer contact route must still be set in Play Console before publication; this repository intentionally does not invent one.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date and the published policy will be updated before the related app release.
