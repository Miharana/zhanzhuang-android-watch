## Design Context

### Users
People practising Zhan Zhuang for 15–180 minutes, often with a Pixel Watch and a paired Android phone. They need a quiet, glanceable timer, dependable interval haptics, optional heart-rate/Health Connect recording, and an obvious way to begin another session without breaking a calm practice state.

### Brand Personality
Grounded, quiet, and refined. The interface should feel steady and contemplative rather than clinical, gamified, loud, or ornamental.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the approved Urticad-derived warm paper, soil, reflective gold, and restrained sparkle system. Phone surfaces are warm and light; Wear surfaces are deep soil for low-distraction viewing. Gold is reserved for identity, selected states, progress, and the primary action. Avoid cyan/purple gradients, gradient text, broad metallic panels, looping effects, generic card grids, and decorative glass.

### Design Principles
- Keep the next practice action visible and reachable, including after completion and at large font scales.
- Let warm paper and soil carry the interface; use gold rarely enough that the primary action remains unmistakable.
- Prefer a small number of strong hierarchy levels, generous breathing room, and 48 dp minimum targets.
- Preserve full function without a watch or health permission, and explain degraded states plainly.
- Use sparkle only once at start or completion, suppressing it for reduced motion, power saver, and ambient contexts.
